The **TH** combination creates two unique sounds that do not exist in Ukrainian. To make them, place the tip of your tongue gently **between your upper and lower teeth**:
1. **Voiceless `/θ/`** — air only (no voice vibration).
2. **Voiced `/ð/`** — throat vibrates with a gentle buzzing feeling.

## 1. Voiceless TH: `/θ/` (Quiet Whisper)

Breathe air gently between your tongue and upper teeth. No vibration in the neck!

| Word | IPA | Ukrainian Meaning | Practice Phrase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Think** | `/θɪŋk/` | Думати | *"I think so."* |
| **Three** | `/θriː/` | Три (число) | *"Three books."* |
| **Thanks** | `/θæŋks/` | Дякую | *"Thanks a lot!"* |
| **Bath** | `/bæθ/` | Ванна | *"Warm bath."* |
| **Birthday** | `/ˈbɝːθ.deɪ/` | День народження | *"Happy birthday!"* |

> [!CAUTION]
> Do not replace `/θ/` with **[s]** (*"sink"* instead of *"think"*) or **[f]** (*"free"* instead of *"three"*).

---

## 2. Voiced TH: `/ð/` (Vibrating Buzz)

Keep your tongue in the exact same spot, but activate your vocal cords. You should feel a gentle buzz in your throat!

| Word | IPA | Ukrainian Meaning | Practice Phrase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **This** | `/ðɪs/` | Цей / Ця / Це | *"This is easy."* |
| **That** | `/ðæt/` | Той / Та / Те | *"I like that."* |
| **The** | `/ðə/` • `/ðiː/` | Означений артикль | *"The sun is shining."* |
| **Mother** | `/ˈmʌð.ɚ/` | Мама | *"My mother."* |
| **Together** | `/təˈɡeð.ɚ/` | Разом | *"We learn together."* |

> [!CAUTION]
> Do not replace `/ð/` with **[z]** (*"ze"* instead of *"the"*) or **[d]** (*"dis"* instead of *"this"*).
