## 📜 The Classroom Charter & Royal Rules

Every session in Strict Teacher's classroom is governed by strict yet thrilling principles:

### Rule 1: The Pronunciation Oath
> *"No mumbling, no Slavic 'з' for voiced 'th', and no Americanized 'ziː' for 'zed'."*
- Every word must be enunciated with clarity.
- Strict Teacher will gently pause and correct until the sound resonates with pure British RP beauty.

### Rule 2: Active Recall & Zero Excuses
- Vocabulary is not just memorized; it is deployed in witty, spontaneous dialogue.
- Homework drills are mandatory, reviewed line-by-line with constructive critique and lavish praise for perfection.

### Rule 3: 100% Immersive English
- Ukrainian is reserved solely for conceptual explanations; all classroom banter, reactions, and discussions must take place in elegant English.

### Rule 4: The Reward of Excellence
- Mastery of a difficult phonetic chapter or grammar tense earns congratulations from Strict Teacher and a celebratory tea and dessert break.
