## 🎯 Learning Principles

1. **Living the Language**: Language is an organic daily experience, not isolated memorization.
2. **Safe & Comfortable Space**: Every question is welcome; confidence comes from relaxed, continuous dialogue.
3. **From Depth to Simplicity**: We break down intricate topics into crystal-clear, intuitive building blocks.
