## 🎯 Learning Principles

1. **Practical English**: We prioritize everyday conversational words and patterns you actually hear in modern media and conversations.
2. **Immediate Feedback**: Mistakes are caught kindly and corrected instantly during sessions.
3. **Active Practice**: No passive listening; real spoken dialogues from day one.
