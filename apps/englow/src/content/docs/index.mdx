The English alphabet has **26 letters**: **5 vowels** (A, E, I, O, U) and **21 consonants**. The letter **Z** is globally recognized as both **Zee** `/ziː/` and **Zed** `/zed/` — both are clearly understood across the world.

Each card below contains the letter, its sound, and **3 practical everyday words** to build your vocabulary from day one.
