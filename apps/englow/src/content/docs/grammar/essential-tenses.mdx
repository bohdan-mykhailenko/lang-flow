English has 12 tense-aspect combinations, but **80% of daily international conversation** relies entirely on 4 primary forms:
1. **Present Simple** (Habits, facts, routines)
2. **Present Continuous** (Actions happening right now, temporary states)
3. **Past Simple** (Finished past events with definite time)
4. **Future with Will / Going to** (Spontaneous decisions vs planned intentions)

---

## 🕒 1. Present Simple vs Present Continuous

| Aspect | Present Simple | Present Continuous |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formula** | `Subject + V1 (s/es)` | `Subject + am/is/are + V-ing` |
| **Usage** | Permanent habits, routines, timeless truths | Happening right now, temporary situations |
| **Time Markers** | *always, usually, every day, often, rarely* | *now, at the moment, right now, currently* |
| **Example** | *"Strict Teacher teaches English every evening."* | *"Настінька is listening attentively right now."* |
| **Negative** | `don't / doesn't + V1` | `am not / isn't / aren't + V-ing` |
| **Question** | `Do / Does + Subject + V1?` | `Am / Is / Are + Subject + V-ing?` |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **State Verbs Rule:**
> Verbs describing feelings, beliefs, or senses (*love, like, know, understand, believe, want*) are rarely used in the continuous `-ing` form:
> - ✅ *"I understand international speakers."*
> - ❌ *"I am understanding international speakers."*

---

## 🏛️ 2. Past Simple: Finished Time

Used for completed actions at a known or implied point in the past (*yesterday, last week, in 2025, ago*).

### Regular Verbs: The 3 Pronunciations of `-ed`
Adding `-ed` to regular verbs creates 3 distinct phonetic endings:
1. **/t/** after voiceless sounds (*p, k, f, s, ʃ, tʃ*): **Looked** `/lʊkt/`, **Kissed** `/kɪst/`
2. **/d/** after voiced sounds (*b, g, v, z, m, n, l, vowels*): **Loved** `/lʌvd/`, **Played** `/pleɪd/`
3. **/ɪd/** after **t** and **d**: **Wanted** `/ˈwɑːn.tɪd/`, **Decided** `/dɪˈsaɪ.dɪd/`

---

## 🚀 3. Expressing the Future

| Structure | Usage | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Will + V1** | Spontaneous decisions made at the moment of speech, offers, predictions without evidence | *"The tea is hot? I will bring some milk."* |
| **Be going to + V1** | Premeditated plans, intentions, predictions with present physical evidence | *"Настінька is going to travel the world and speak fluent English."* |
| **Present Continuous** | Fixed calendar arrangements with other people | *"We are meeting for our 1-on-1 session at 6 PM."* |
