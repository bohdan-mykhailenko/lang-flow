Irregular verbs do not follow the standard `+ed` rule. Instead, they preserve ancient Germanic vowel mutations (*Ablaut*). In everyday speech, irregular verbs represent **over 70% of all verb occurrences**.

Below is Strict Teacher's essential high-frequency reference table:

---

## 📋 The Essential 25 Irregular Verbs

| Base Form (V1) | Past Simple (V2) | Past Participle (V3) | Meaning | Strict Teacher's Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Be** `/biː/` | **Was / Were** `/wɒz/` | **Been** `/biːn/` | To exist / state | *"I have been in London twice."* |
| **Become** `/bɪˈkʌm/` | **Became** `/bɪˈkeɪm/` | **Become** `/bɪˈkʌm/` | To grow into | *"She became fluent rapidly."* |
| **Begin** `/bɪˈɡɪn/` | **Began** `/bɪˈɡæn/` | **Begun** `/bɪˈɡʌn/` | To start | *"Our lesson began on time."* |
| **Break** `/breɪk/` | **Broke** `/brəʊk/` | **Broken** `/ˈbrəʊ.kən/` | To fracture | *"Never break the classroom rule."* |

| **Drive** `/draɪv/` | **Drove** `/drəʊv/` | **Driven** `/ˈdrɪv.ən/` | To operate vehicle | *"He drove along Oxford Street."* |

| **Find** `/faɪnd/` | **Found** `/faʊnd/` | **Found** `/faʊnd/` | To discover | *"We found the perfect solution."* |
| **Give** `/ɡɪv/` | **Gave** `/ɡeɪv/` | **Given** `/ˈɡɪv.ən/` | To bestow upon | *"He gave her high praise."* |

| **Have** `/hæv/` | **Had** `/hæd/` | **Had** `/hæd/` | To possess | *"We had a wonderful conversation."* |
| **Know** `/nəʊ/` | **Knew** `/njuː/` | **Known** `/nəʊn/` | To be aware of | *"I knew the rule instinctively."* |
| **Make** `/meɪk/` | **Made** `/meɪd/` | **Made** `/meɪd/` | To craft, create | *"She made tremendous progress."* |
